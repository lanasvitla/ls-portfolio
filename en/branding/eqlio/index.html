Benchmark analysis

People want to join sports challenges – without feeling destined to lose

As part of the benchmark analysis, I compared YuMuuv, Pacer for Teams, Wellable and MoveSpring, focusing on challenge mechanics, fair rankings and tools for administrators.

Unequal conditions

Absolute metrics favor experienced participants and quickly demotivate newcomers.

Motivation drops

When a goal feels unattainable, interest in the competition fades after the first few days.

Lack of overview

HR teams struggle to quickly assess participant activity and identify who needs additional support.

Hypothesis and solution direction

Competition motivates when progress feels personal and the conditions – fair

If results are measured relative to each participant’s starting level, more employees will stay engaged in challenges regardless of fitness level.

Personal progress

Goals and progress are measured against the starting level, not only absolute metrics.

Team support

Shared challenges, team results and support from colleagues help sustain regular participation.

Transparent management

The administrator sees engagement, progress and problem areas in one dashboard.
